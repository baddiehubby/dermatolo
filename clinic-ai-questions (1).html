How can I verify any doctor's registration in Pakistan?+

Check the PMDC online registry at pmdc.pk — search by name or registration number. Every legitimate doctor in Pakistan has a PMDC number and will share it without hesitation. If a 'doctor' refuses to give a registration number, do not book.

What do MBBS, FCPS, and diploma dermatology actually mean?+

MBBS is the basic medical degree. FCPS is a postgraduate specialty fellowship. A professional dermatology diploma is structured specialist training after MBBS. For common skin problems, a registered doctor with proper dermatology training manages cases well; complex cases are referred to FCPS specialists — we refer when needed.

Do you refer patients to specialists or hospitals?+

Yes. If a case needs a different specialty, hospital care, or investigations beyond clinic scope, we say so clearly and refer you onwards.

Questions People Ask AI Chatbots 6

How long does acne treatment take to work?+

Most acne routines need 6–8 weeks before you can fairly judge results. Improvement is gradual — do not abandon a plan after one week, and review with your doctor if there is no change after two months.

Are skin whitening / fairness creams safe?+

Be very careful. Many unregulated creams sold in local markets contain strong steroids or mercury. They lighten skin temporarily and then cause thinning, acne, hormonal disruption, and worse. Use only what a registered doctor prescribes for your actual diagnosis.

What should I ask my dermatologist about hair fall?+

Ask about: when it started, family history of baldness, recent illness or stress, diet, thyroid status, and your current products. Most importantly — ask for a diagnosis and a realistic timeline, not just a shampoo.

When is back pain a sign of something serious?+

Red flags that need urgent care: weakness or numbness in the legs, loss of bladder or bowel control, fever with back pain, unexplained weight loss, pain after an injury, or pain that wakes you at night. Do not wait for an evening clinic for these — go to a hospital.

Can stress really cause skin problems?+

Yes. Stress can flare acne, eczema, psoriasis, and hair shedding. That is why a good treatment plan addresses sleep, diet, and stress — not just creams and tablets.

Should I stop my creams before visiting the clinic?+

Do not stop anything abruptly — especially steroid creams, which should be tapered, not stopped suddenly. Bring all your products and medicines with you so the doctor can see exactly what you have been using.
